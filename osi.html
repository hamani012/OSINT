SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & ETHICS

Automotive Spare Parts OSINT Methodology

The Automotive Spare Parts OSINT Tool centralizes publicly available listing metadata across online auto stores, salvage classifieds, and authorized catalogs—enabling mechanics, fleet operators, and researchers to verify OEM compatibility and benchmark market prices.

01

Step 1: Collect Information

Gathers structured listings from public automotive marketplaces, online catalogs, and permitted APIs while respecting rate limits and robots.txt.

02

Step 2: Organize the Data

Normalizes unstructured ad text into standardized database fields: OEM Part Number, Manufacturer, Condition, Seller Public Contact, Timestamp, and Source URL.

03

Step 3: Compare Listings

Identifies duplicate or cross-listed parts via OEM code clustering, computes Lowest/Median/Highest price benchmarks, and tracks longitudinal price changes.

04

Step 4: Display Results

Delivers actionable intelligence via an interactive dashboard with instant filtering, side-by-side duplicate inspection, price drop alerts, and CSV export.

Privacy, Reliability & OSINT Governance

This system strictly indexes publicly accessible commercial listings and authorized data feeds. Because third-party marketplace listings may contain outdated prices or incomplete fitment notes, every record preserves its First Seen and Last Observed timestamps alongside a direct link to the original source advertisement for human verification.
